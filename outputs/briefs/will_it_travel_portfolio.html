Will It Travel?

Which US shows will succeed in Australia, and on which kind of platform? A data tool that tells a TV sales team which NBCUniversal titles to sell to which Australian channel or streaming service.

The question

A US hit doesn't automatically work in Australia

Distributors license US content to Australian buyers. To pitch well, a sales team needs to know which genres and formats actually find an Australian audience, and which of three very different buyer groups each title suits.

Group 1

Free-to-air TV

Seven, Nine, Ten, ABC, SBS

Group 2

Free streaming apps (BVOD)

7plus, 9Now, 10 Play, ABC iview, SBS On Demand

Group 3

Paid streamers

Stan, Foxtel/BINGE, Netflix, Prime Video

Key findings

Four things a sales team should know

Finding 01

Free TV buys US crime procedurals, and almost nothing else

US content wins just 2.6% of free-to-air Top 30 slots and 5.4% on the free streaming apps. Half the chart is Australian news, sport and live events. The US shows that do break through are scripted crime series such as The Rookie and 9-1-1.

Finding 02

Series travel to free TV; films travel to paid streaming

The travel score compares a format's share of Australian chart space with its share in the US. 100 means it performs the same in both markets. US scripted series score 343 on free-to-air, while US films score 9. On paid streaming the picture flips: documentary (138) and animated film (115) over-perform, and reality (50) lags.

Finding 03

On paid streaming, being a US hit isn't enough

Netflix originals launch globally, so they almost always travel. Licensed US hits need a local deal, a local home and local promotion, and most never chart in Australia. Documentary is the exception.

Finding 04

Most NBCU hits are already licensed. The bigger win is renewals

31 of the 35 NBCUniversal titles that charted in the US but not in Australia already have an Australian streaming home. The true white space is small. The larger opportunity is a renewal watch-list: 30 NBCU titles licensed here that never chart. Four of them fit free-to-air better than their current home.

AI in the workflow

Pitch briefs written by Claude, grounded in the data

For each platform group, the Claude API drafts a one-page pitch brief from a data pack of the project's own numbers, with an instruction not to invent figures. Every brief ends with its caveats and a "check before sending" note.

Free-to-air

Lead with crime series, not films

US content is a small slice of free TV, and what charts is crime and procedural drama. Pitch The Endgame with a crime renewal such as Law & Order, and start with Seven, which already carries both Law & Order and The Hunting Party on 7plus.

Free streaming apps

Crime does 3× better here than in the US

US shows take about 5% of chart space on 7plus, 9Now and the other apps, but US crime and thriller series score 296. Package scripted crime series and pitch for discovery, not volume.

Paid streamers

Family animation and true-crime documentary

Both lanes over-perform in Australia, and the strongest unplaced titles sit in them: The Super Mario Galaxy Movie and Homicide Squad New Orleans, followed by DreamWorks and Illumination animation.

How it works

Method

  1. Collect Nielsen Streaming Top 10, Netflix Top 10 (US and Australia) and 361 days of OzTAM VOZ Top 30 into one tidy table.
  2. Use rankings, not audiences. Nielsen measures minutes watched and OzTAM measures average viewers, so each chart position becomes a rank percentile within its own chart.
  3. Enrich every title through the TMDB API with genre, format, origin and production company, and flag NBCUniversal-owned studios.
  4. Match US and Australian titles, link local remakes to US formats, and review uncertain matches by hand.
  5. Score each genre and format by platform group, then rank NBCU candidates and check Australian availability through JustWatch.
Travel score
(share of AU chart points) ÷ (share of US chart points) × 100

Chart points are the sum of rank percentiles. Segments with fewer than three Australian titles are not scored.

Built with

Python (pandas), Excel (PivotTable-ready workbook), PowerPoint (python-pptx), Claude API, TMDB API, Claude Code

Limitations

  • Free TV scores rest on about 10 US titles in a year. Treat them as direction, not precision.
  • Stan, BINGE and Prime Video publish no charts, so Netflix Top 10 Australia stands in for paid streaming.
  • The Nielsen sample covers 13 of 52 weeks. Netflix US (52 weeks) is the main like-for-like US signal.
  • Top 10 and Top 30 charts only show hits. Rights and availability should be confirmed against contracts.
