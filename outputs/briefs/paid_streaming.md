# Paid streamers (Stan, Foxtel/BINGE, Netflix, Prime Video): pitch brief

**Bottom line:** Lead with family animation and true-crime documentary. Both over-perform in Australia, and our strongest unplaced titles sit in those lanes. The data for this group is thin because only Netflix publishes Australian charts. Use it as a directional guide, not proof.

## What this audience buys

**How to read the travel score:** 100 means US content gets the same share of chart space in Australia as it does in the US. Above 100 means it over-performs in Australia. Below 100 means it under-performs.

- **US content dominates.** It holds 71% of Australian chart slots. UK content holds 10.1% and Australian content 3.7%.
- **Formats that travel (Netflix AU):**
  - Documentary: **138**
  - Animated film: **115**
  - Film overall: **112**
- **Formats that don't travel:**
  - Scripted series: 94
  - Limited series: 86
  - Reality: 50
  - Animated series: 42
- **Genres that travel:**
  - Romance: 137, but based on only 4 titles
  - Horror: 134
  - Comedy: 118
  - Documentary: 116
  - Family & Animation: 114
- **Genres that don't travel:**
  - Drama: 65
  - Reality: 50
- **Licensed titles struggle.** 91% of Netflix originals that hit in the US also chart in Australia. Only 28% of licensed US hits do.
- **Documentary is the exception.** 70.8% of licensed US documentary hits also chart in Australia. Other genres:
  - Family & Animation: 32.1%
  - Horror: 29.4%
  - Action & Adventure: 6.2%

## How to pitch it

- **Family animation package for BINGE or Netflix.** Bundle Super Mario Galaxy, Migration and Ruby Gillman. Pair the package with the renewal of our animated library already on BINGE. Animated *film* travels (115), while animated *series* does not (42), so lead with features.
- **True-crime documentary for Netflix.** Homicide Squad New Orleans is the safest licensed bet in the data, with a 70.8% pickup rate for licensed documentary hits. Position it alongside the Australian true-crime comparables listed above.
- **Genre film slate for Stan or Prime Video.** Offer Renfield (horror, 134), Fast X and Straight Outta Compton. Lead with horror and keep the drama angle low-key.
- **Avoid leading with** reality, drama series or animated series. All three score well below 100.

## Caveats

- Stan, BINGE and Prime Video have no public charts. Netflix Top 10 Australia stands in for the whole group.
- Format scores are measured on Netflix AU. The pack doesn't say which platform the genre scores come from.
- Some scores rest on small samples: Romance has 4 Australian titles and Animated series has 5.
- Tier A titles each had only 1 week in a US Top 10. The Nielsen data is a 13-week sample.
- Tier B rests on TMDB popularity, not ratings.
- Peacock originals are excluded because Stan's NBCUniversal output deal already covers them.
